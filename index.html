Since 2018

A roastery that happens to have a café attached

We started with one second-hand roaster and a stall at the Saturday market. Seven years later the roaster is bigger, the queue is longer, and the rule has not changed: if we would not drink it ourselves, we do not sell it.

Everything on the bar is roasted upstairs. The pastries are laminated overnight in the back kitchen. The blue on the walls is the same blue on the cups, which was either good branding or a paint sale — the story changes depending on who you ask.
